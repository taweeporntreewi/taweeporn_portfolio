Overview

JP Carry is a cross-border logistics platform that simplifies proxy buying and parcel forwarding between Japan and Thailand. It includes a customer portal for placing service requests and tracking orders, and an admin dashboard for managing daily operations.

As the sole UX/UI Designer, I led the entire design process from research and problem definition to interface design, usability testing and developer handoff, working closely with the team and developers throughout.

Key challenges

01

An end-to-end service experience

Creating a seamless experience from placing a request to tracking deliveries and managing an account.

02

Turning business requirements into user flows

Turning business requirements into practical workflows and clear interfaces.

03

Two different user roles

Designing for both customers and administrators while keeping the platform consistent and easy to use.

My responsibilities

  • Led the end-to-end design process from discovery to developer handoff
  • Ran research and defined product requirements from business needs
  • Created information architecture, user flows and site maps
  • Designed paper prototypes, wireframes and interactive prototypes
  • Designed responsive interfaces for customers and administrators
  • Built a reusable UI library for visual consistency
  • Designed the brand identity, logo and key visual assets
  • Ran usability testing and refined the designs from feedback
  • Collaborated closely with developers through implementation

Design process

Discover

Understanding the problem through research, assumptions and user insights.

Define

Turning research findings into actionable product goals.

Develop

Structuring the product experience before moving into visual design.

Deliver

Turning concepts into interfaces, validating the designs and preparing them for implementation, including a logo and visual identity for a consistent brand presence.

Final solution

A customer portal that takes users from request to delivery, and an admin dashboard for managing daily operations.

CUSTOMER PORTAL

Home

Introduces the platform's services with quick access to parcel forwarding, proxy buying and shipment tracking.

Services

Presents the main services, Proxy Buy and Drop Off, with a clear and straightforward flow.

Summary

Summarizes all purchase details before submission: products, pricing, shipping fees and order information in one place.

Payment

Pay the first part by QR code, with a tracker that shows every step from placing the order to picking up the parcel.

Wallet

JPCarry Pay wallet for top-ups, transfers and saved cards, with daily check-in coins that reward returning users.

Check parcel status

Track parcel progress and view shipping updates in real time.

Member

Personal information, wallet, orders and account management in one place.

ADMIN DASHBOARD

Overview

An overview of daily operations, order status and key platform activities.

Bulk update list

Update multiple orders at once, improving efficiency and cutting repetitive tasks.

Order detail modal

Review and update each order in a modal without leaving the current page.

MOBILE

Responsive screens

The customer portal adapts to mobile with a bottom navigation bar, keeping services, payments, wallet and account easy to reach with one hand.
