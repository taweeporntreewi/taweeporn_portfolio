Overview

Homnect24 is a CRM platform built for real estate professionals to manage leads, properties, contracts, bookings and team collaboration in one place.

As the UX/UI Designer, I worked closely with a product manager and developers to translate business and user requirements into easy-to-use experiences. My work included designing user flows, wireframes, responsive UI, and maintaining a scalable design system throughout the product lifecycle.

Key challenges

01

Complex business workflows

Lead management, property listings, contracts, bookings and team collaboration live in a single platform. The challenge was to bring them together in one clear, simple experience.

02

Designing for scalability

As the product grew, new features and modules needed to work well with the existing interface while staying consistent across the platform.

03

Cross-functional collaboration

Requirements evolved through ongoing discussions with the product manager and developers, so the design had to turn changing needs into practical solutions.

My responsibilities

  • Collaborated with a product manager to refine product requirements
  • Designed user flows and wireframes
  • Created user interface designs
  • Built and maintained the design system
  • Designed responsive layouts
  • Worked closely with developers throughout implementation
  • Ran visual QA so builds matched the designs across devices
  • Refined designs based on stakeholder feedback

Design system

To keep the product consistent, I built and maintained a scalable design system with reusable components, color styles, typography, spacing and interaction patterns.
